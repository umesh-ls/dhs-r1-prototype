Worth trying

  • Raise a dak. New Dak → pick a Document Type and Subject. The path, the form and who decides are settled by the topic, not by the person raising it.
  • Watch the value decide the office. On an SCI-15, mark items capital or revenue and change the cost — the approving office changes as you type. Nothing is approved at Satsang Place or Area level; what is approved above the Zonal limit is still to be settled with Beas.
  • Reply and forward. Open a dak in the Inbox. Recipients are limited to what the dak's own route allows; copies are read-only and never enter anyone's pending list.
  • Fill an SCI form. SCI Forms → new. Use Side by side to watch the official sheet fill in as you type, then open it at actual size.
  • Approve something. Any approval asks for the one-time code — 123456. The moment it is given the system writes the Approval Letter and a duly approved copy of every SCI form. The letter sets out the whole of what was approved — it is the paper handed to the committee — over the stamp of the approving authority; the form stays on record, carrying the stamp of every authority in the line. Open them from the dak, or from Approved Records.
  • Change the rules. As DHS Admin, open Topics & Workflows and edit a topic. Tick value-based to see the approval limits themselves — change a figure, publish, and routing follows at once. Dak already in flight keep the version they started under.

Everything resets when you reload the page. Feedback is welcome on anything — wording, sequence, what is missing, and above all whether the routing matches how the offices actually work.

New in this revision

  • An approval writes its own papers. The moment a decision is recorded, the system issues the Approval Letter and a duly approved copy of every SCI form enclosed — nobody drafts either. Both are kept permanently in Approved Records.
  • The letter carries the decision itself. It is the paper handed to the committee, so on an SCI-52 it sets out who is appointed and on whose bio-data, who continues, who stands relieved, and the committee as it now reads. The form stays on the office record.
  • Every authority in the line is stamped. One stamp per office, in the order the dak travelled, each naming a person, a designation and a date. The Approval Letter bears the final approving authority's stamp alone.
  • The sheets in the decks are shown at full size — they are the prototype's own A4 pages, so what is on the slide is what the office would hold.
  • A dak is numbered from its topic, not from a form. The dak takes L or C; every SCI form keeps an F number of its own and binds to the dak without either altering.
